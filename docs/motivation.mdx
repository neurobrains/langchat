## The Problem

Building AI chatbots is harder than it should be. You need:

- Multiple APIs to integrate (OpenAI, Pinecone, Supabase)
- Complex error handling and retries
- Chat history management
- Vector search setup
- Days or weeks of development time

## The Solution

**LangChat handles all of this for you.**

Instead of weeks of work, get a production-ready chatbot in minutes.
